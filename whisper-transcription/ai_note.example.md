# Note for the AI analyzing these transcripts

The `.md` files in this folder are automatic speech-to-text transcripts generated
with Whisper (see the `model` field in each file's front matter). Expect errors:
misheard words, wrong or split acronyms, technical terms and proper names,
misspelled speaker names, and passages degraded by accents or audio quality.
Infer the intended meaning from context, flag ambiguous passages instead of
guessing silently, and do not treat any sentence as a verbatim quote.
Timestamps are approximate.
